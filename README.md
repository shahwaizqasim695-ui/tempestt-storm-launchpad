# Tempestt Lyles Official

Build a premium, modern book marketing website for the novel series "Mastersippi J2 – Jessica: The Storm Collection" by author Tempestt Lyles, published by Parker Publishers.

Build the homepage first.

### Book Details:
- Full Title: Mastersippi J2 – Jessica (The Storm Collection)
- Author: Tempestt Lyles
- Series: The Storm Collection / The Book of Jessica (3-book series)
- Genre: Dark Fiction / Emotional Drama / Psychological Journey
- Publisher: Parker Publishers
- ISBN: 978-1-963456-78-0
- Tone: Dark, intense, emotional, transformative, with themes of betrayal, healing, identity, family secrets, toxic relationships, and personal rebirth.

### Visual Style (Very Important):
The design must be inspired by the official book cover:
- Dark, cinematic, dramatic atmosphere
- Color palette: Deep blacks, charcoal, burnt orange/red fire tones, metallic copper/gold accents, subtle blue highlights
- Moody, high-contrast lighting (like fire against dark sky)
- Elegant serif + modern sans-serif typography mix
- Luxury book-launch feel (think high-end fantasy/thriller book sites)
- Use the book cover image as the primary visual asset (hero section, about book, etc.)
- Subtle particle/fire/smoke effects or glowing orb-inspired accents (keep performance-friendly)

### Homepage Requirements:
1. Cinematic full-screen hero with book cover, strong headline + short emotional tagline, "Read the Series" / "Get the Book" CTA buttons, and subtle glowing/particle atmospheric effects
2. Featured quote or short synopsis teaser
3. Author section with author photo, bio, and personal note from Tempestt Lyles (as found on the back cover)
4. Series overview teaser for "The Storm Collection" (3-book collection, starting with Jessica)
5. Prominent call-to-action / retailer purchase links & newsletter signup
6. Sticky navigation and atmospheric footer with Parker Publishers branding and ISBN
